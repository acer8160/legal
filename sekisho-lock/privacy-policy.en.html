Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sekisho Lock (the “App”) handles user information. The developer and operator of the App (“we”) respects users’ privacy and follows a basic policy of collecting only the minimum information necessary.

1. Basic Policy

The App does not collect personally identifiable information (personal information) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The App also does not perform usage analytics. The only information transmitted off your device is the purchase information required to process billing.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Purchase Information

To process and manage the purchase, restoration, and status of “Sekisho Premium,” we handle purchase transaction information (such as whether a purchase was made, the plan type, and the subscription expiration date) through Apple and our billing infrastructure provider (RevenueCat). This information is managed under an anonymous identifier that cannot identify you, and is not linked to personal information such as your name. Payment information such as credit card numbers is processed by Apple, and we do not receive it.

2.2 Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • The flashcards and decks you create, and settings such as the number of questions, are stored only in the database on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Your selection of blocked targets and Screen Time related information are processed on your device by Apple’s framework. We never obtain information such as which apps you block or which apps you use and when.
  • The App only sends local notifications that are generated and delivered entirely on your device; it does not use push notifications that pass through external servers.

3. Purposes of Use

We use the information we handle only for the following purposes:

  • Providing the App’s features, improving quality and convenience, and identifying issues
  • Providing and managing “Sekisho Premium” (purchase, renewal, restoration)

4. Provision to Third Parties and Service Providers

We do not sell or provide the information we handle to third parties without the user’s consent. However, to the extent necessary to operate the App, we use the following service providers:

ProviderPurposeInformation handled
Apple Inc.App distribution and payment processingPurchase and payment information
RevenueCat, Inc.Purchase status management (subscriptions and the lifetime plan)Purchase status linked to an anonymous ID

Please refer to each provider’s official website for their respective privacy policies.

5. Tracking

We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use the information we handle for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. If you have any question or request regarding your privacy, please contact us using the form in Section 10.

6. Data Storage Location

The purchase status linked to an anonymous ID is stored on our service provider’s (RevenueCat, Inc.) servers located in the United States.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a wide range of users including students, and does not collect personal information from users of any age.

8. Security

We and our service providers take reasonable security measures to prevent the leakage, loss, or alteration of the information we handle.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will update the effective date on this page to notify you.

10. Contact

For inquiries or feedback regarding this Policy, please contact us via the form below (no Google account login is required).
