Privacy Policy (Waitlist Page)

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on the waitlist page for Say It In Japan (the "Page", https://kaedeyamazaki.github.io/japan-travel-phrases-lp/). The developer and operator of Say It In Japan ("we") respects visitors' privacy and follows a basic policy of collecting only the minimum information necessary.

This Policy covers the Page only. A separate privacy policy will be provided for the Say It In Japan iPhone app when it is released.

1. Basic Policy

The Page collects your email address only if you choose to join the waitlist. We do not ask for your name, phone number, or address.

To understand how visitors find and use the Page, we also collect anonymous usage statistics that do not identify you. We do not use cookies for this purpose.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Email Address (only if you join the waitlist)

When you submit the waitlist form, we receive:

  • Your email address
  • How you reached the Page (campaign information included in the link, such as "reddit")
  • When you plan to visit Japan (e.g. "Mar – Apr 2027"), only if you answer the optional question shown after sign-up

2.2 Anonymous Usage Statistics

When you view the Page, we record anonymous events such as viewing the Page, scrolling to the pricing table, selecting the email field, submitting the form, and answering or skipping the trip-timing question, together with general technical information (browser type, operating system, screen size, referring website, and campaign information in the link).

  • We do not use cookies or similar storage for these statistics. A temporary identifier exists only while the Page is open and is discarded when you close it.
  • Your IP address is discarded by our analytics provider and is not stored.
  • Your email address is never sent to our analytics provider.

2.3 Inquiries (only if you submit the contact form)

When you submit the contact form, we receive the email address you enter and the content of your inquiry.

3. Purposes of Use

We use the information we handle only for the following purposes:

  • Sending you one email when the app is released (waitlist members only)
  • Understanding interest in the app and improving the Page (aggregate statistics only)
  • Deciding when to release the app, based on when waitlist members plan to travel (aggregate only)
  • Responding to inquiries and deletion requests

We do not send newsletters or marketing emails other than the release announcement.

4. Provision to Third Parties and Service Providers

We do not sell or provide the information we handle to third parties without your consent. However, to the extent necessary to operate the Page, we use the following service providers:

Provider Purpose Information handled
Google LLC (Google Forms) Receiving and storing waitlist sign-ups and inquiries Email address, campaign information, trip timing (if answered), content of inquiries
PostHog Inc. Anonymous usage statistics Anonymous events and technical information (IP address discarded)
GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) Hosting the Page Access logs processed by GitHub as the hosting provider
Google LLC (Google Fonts) Displaying web fonts Information sent by your browser when loading fonts

Please refer to each provider's official website for their respective privacy policies.

5. Tracking

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use the information we handle for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

6. Data Storage Location

  • Waitlist sign-ups and inquiries are stored on Google's servers (Google Forms).
  • Anonymous usage statistics are stored on PostHog's servers in the European Union.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • We keep your email address and the information registered with it until we send the release announcement, and delete them within one month after sending it.
  • If we decide not to release the app, we will not send an announcement and will delete them within one month of that decision.
  • We delete the content of inquiries within one month after we finish responding.
  • You can ask us to delete your email address at any time using the contact method in Section 11, or by replying to the release announcement email. We will delete it without undue delay.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK, or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact method in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

The Page is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will update the effective date on this page to notify you.

11. Contact

For inquiries or requests regarding this Policy, please contact us via the form below (no Google account login is required).
